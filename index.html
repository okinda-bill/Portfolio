Employer partnerships and placements

Generation Kenya trains unemployed young people in digital marketing and places them with employers at no cost to the employer. My job is to make that placement happen.

I find employers who are hiring, understand what each role really needs, and put forward graduates who can do the work. Then I stay with both sides until the person is placed and settled.

Recent partners include NGOs, a coffee brand, a flexible-workspace company and consulting firms, hiring for marketing, research, field and call-centre roles.

How a placement happens

  1. Find the right employers

    Research and outreach through Apollo.io, LinkedIn and referrals, aimed at companies with real entry-level demand.

  2. Understand the role

    Turn the employer's brief into a clear job post and a list of what a strong candidate looks like.

  3. Shortlist

    Match graduates to the role and check each one against our placement tracker before anyone is put forward.

  4. Assess and interview

    Run assessments and mock interviews, then schedule employer interviews for the shortlist.

  5. Place and follow up

    Close the hire and keep checking in with the graduate and the employer after they start.
